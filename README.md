# StickFighters

A stick-figure beat-'em-up: you're the orange fighter holding off waves of the pink army. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, so GitHub Pages serves it as-is.

**Play:** https://rhetters.github.io/stickfighters/

This page is the player's guide. What's built and what's next is in [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md).

## Modes

The title says **PRESS START**: press any key, button or tap. The menu is pictures: **SOLO** (Story or Arcade), **MULTIPLAYER** (Local, Host or Join) and **CUSTOMIZE** (Character or House) each open a page of their own; **TRAINING** and **SETTINGS** sit under them. Pictures stay grey until you highlight one.

- **Solo → Story** opens the Story screen: pick a theme by its picture (a ★ CLEARED mark once you've beaten it), or **?** for a surprise theme that earns 10% more XP and ninja stars. Every run starts with the pencil drawing your fighters in. **Castle Siege** is a new run every time, built from a random seed (set your own in Settings to replay one): the Wooded Trail, the Sewer Tunnels (a slippery floor) and the Castle Keep, about three minutes each, with a Captain at the end of the first two. Between them you're carried on at random: launched by a catapult, sliding down a pipe, swinging on a rope over a chasm, or smashing the castle gate. Once in a while a mini-boss leaps in and knocks everyone flat. At the end, the Pink General is drawn in by a pencil and comes to life. **Pirate Cove** goes from the Sunny Beach to the Pirate Ship (where a cannon can fire everyone onward) to the Treasure Cave, where Captain Pinkbeard leaps in. **Mountain** climbs from the Alpine Meadow up the Mountainside (the path goes uphill between fights, steeper the higher you get) to the Snowy Peak, by balloon, gondola, cliff ladder or rope, and the Pink Yeti leaps down at the end. **Space** starts at the Launch Base, where you jump up the platforms of the launch tower to board the rocket. After a fight through the rocket you land on the Moon, where gravity is low and everyone wears a bubble helmet and the Pink Alien is beamed down from a flying saucer. **Volcano** starts in the Tiki Jungle, where steam vents blast whoever stands on them high into the air (a super jump for you; enemies go flying). On the Ashy Slopes, lava rocks fall during fights: move away from the glowing mark. Then you jump into the Crater and climb down rock ledges over lava. Every fight there has ledges, and the pink army follows you up and down them. Lava bounces you out with a little damage but never knocks you out. The Pink Tiki bursts out of the lava. At his last bit of health he stops the fight and the lava starts to rise: climb the ledges out of the crater and finish him at the rim. Each fight you win is a checkpoint, so a KO lets you continue from there. The blow that beats a theme's boss plays in slow motion and knocks out every enemy still standing. Then the victory screen shows each player's victory pose, the damage they did and the damage they took. Tap your ready stamp (a red ✘ turns into a green ✔); when everyone is ready, the theme's picture with **+1** written in its corner (+2…) starts the same theme again, newly built and harder. Or go back to **STORY** (**PLAYERS** in local co-op, **ROOM** online) or to the **MENU**.
- **Solo → Arcade** is the original game: waves on one screen, with a Captain every fifth wave.
- **Difficulty:** EASY, NORMAL or HARD under the Solo pictures (and in the rooms, where the host picks) for Story and Arcade. Easy enemies have less health, hit softer and slower, fewer attack at once, revives are quicker and you get more health back after each fight; Hard is the other way.
- **Solo → Learn** is a short tutorial in the Training Room: a card shows what to do and which button to press (move, jump, punch, kick, a combo, a Surge run, a grab). It's also offered the first time you start Story or Arcade.
- **Multiplayer → Local** opens the Players screen, laid out like an online room with a **LOCAL** sticky note (− and + set 2 to 4 places). Controllers join by pressing any button (B leaves); tap the hand or keyboard picture on an empty place to join with touch or the keyboard; a second keyboard player presses 0 on the number pad. Each player's photo shows their fighter (◀ ▶ to change), what they play with, and buttons for colour, hat and leaving. Pick the mode, **STORY** (which story under it), **ARCADE** or **VERSUS** (best of 3 rounds in The Clearing; free-for-all or teams under it, tap TEAM on a photo to switch sides; grab another player with punch + kick, mash to break free), then START. In co-op, stand next to a downed teammate for 2 seconds to revive them.
- **In a game** each player has a bar across the top: their name, their head in a ring that fills with XP towards the next level (the level number sits in the ring's bottom-right corner), five hearts of health, the yellow Surge bar under them, and the ultimate meter standing on the left (it fills up, then flashes when the ultimate is ready). Your ninja stars are in the top right, beside the pause button.
- **Multiplayer → Online** plays house to house: one player taps **HOST** and shares the room code on the sticky note (a word and a number); others tap **JOIN**, pick the code with arrows and join. The host sets the room size with − and + (2 to 6 players), one per device, PC, iPad and phone together. Each player's photo shows what they play with. The host picks the mode (Story, Arcade, Versus or the host's house) and, under it, which story (or ?) or free-for-all / teams (tap TEAM on your photo to switch). Tap your ready stamp; when everyone is ready, the host can press START. If the host changes anything, everyone readies up again. Online, each screen follows its own fighter: in a story you can stay behind as long as you like, every fight or scene brings everyone in, and an arrow at the edge shows where a teammate is. Every device needs the same version of the game, so reload the page on all of them after an update.
- **Customize → Character** holds four saved fighters. Your fighter stands in a polaroid: tap the name under the photo to change it, the dice for a random look, and the star on the corner for **Skills** (it glows when you have points to spend). The store is pictures: pick a part of the body (**Body**, **Colors**, **Head**, **Neck**, **Upper Body**, **Lower Body**, **Surge**, **Poses**), then a kind (for example Lower Body → Pants, Shorts, Skirts, Dresses or Shoes), then an item; **BACK** (or B on a controller) goes up a step. Body has height, head size, head type (**Donut**, a ring, or **Full**, filled in), line thickness, stance and Delete. Poses go on your pose wheel (up to 8; the first is your victory pose). Each shelf is also the store: tap an item to try it on, tap again to buy it with ninja stars. Defeated enemies drop the stars, and Captains drop gifts. Everything is looks only and never changes how you fight. The treasure chest in the bottom right corner opens the code screen: tap the four squares to change their pictures and press REDEEM; a right code gives you a secret item. In multiplayer, each player picks a fighter on the Players screen and can change their color and hat.
- **Customize → House** builds your house, as big as you like (even a little city): walls square by square, **barriers** (floors, side walls, stairs, spiral stairs and ladders, drawn as black lines), furniture and decorations piece by piece (each costs a few ninja stars), and a background. A wall painted on stairs fills just the steps. The D-pad moves around your land, which grows as you build, and **−** / **+** zoom out and in. Erase gives back every star, front first. **WALK AROUND** lets your fighter explore it: jump onto floors; walking keeps you on your level, holding **Up** while walking takes stairs going up that way and **Down** takes stairs going down; on ladders and spiral stairs **Up** climbs and **Down** goes down; press **Down + Jump** to drop through a floor; side walls stop you; **Esc** (or Start / II) goes straight back to the builder. With several players on one device the camera zooms out to keep everyone on screen; online, each screen follows its own player. Online, the host can pick **HOST'S HOUSE** so everyone visits.
- **Skills** (the star on the Character screen's polaroid): each saved fighter earns XP for every enemy beaten (everyone in the fight gets it) and for clearing levels and Arcade waves, and levels up to 30. Each level gives a skill point (bonus points at 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30) to spend in five branches: Fists, Legs, Surge, Body and Fortune. New fighters start with the basic moves; points unlock the rest and add small boosts. Tap a skill to see what it does and tap it again (or + ADD) to spend a point; TAKE BACK and RESET give points back for free. Each fighting branch ends in an **ULTIMATE** row: buy its first step at level 5, the next at 15 and the last at 30 (Legs ends with the Tornado Kick). You can have one branch's ultimate at a time; take those points back to switch. Leveling up mid-fight shows a golden pillar of light. In Versus your moves and ultimate count, but boosts don't, so a high level can't overpower a low one. A TEST row at the bottom (temporary, for testing) sets the level.
- **Settings:** **Music** and **Sound** volumes (the music changes with the place, gets louder in a fight and has a boss tune), **Colour help** (each player's number over their head, and a ▲ or ● for their team in team Versus), **Bigger text**, and Reduce screen shake. **Pause → MOVES** shows every move.
- **Back up your save** in Settings → Save: copy a code or save a file, and restore it on any device.
- **Training Room** has dummies, hitboxes, frame data and the Feel / Moves / Info panels.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or ← / → | Stick left/right | Left stick / d-pad |
| Aim up / down (picks the move) | W / S or ↑ / ↓ | Stick up/down | Left stick / d-pad |
| Jump (twice for a flip; Down + Jump drops through a floor) | Space | JUMP | A |
| Punch / Kick | J / K | PUNCH / KICK | X / B |
| Surge (hold, or tap on/off: Settings → Surge button): run through enemies, super jump, Surge moves | Shift | SURGE | Bumpers / triggers |
| Block (hits from the front cost Surge instead of health; drops when Surge runs out) | Shift + S, standing still | SURGE + stick down | Bumper + d-pad down |
| Grab | J + K together | PUNCH + KICK | X + B |
| Ultimate (blue meter full; pick it on the Skills screen) | U | ULT | Y |
| Pose wheel (hold, aim, let go) | T | POSE (hold, aim with the stick) | Select / View / − |
| Pause | Esc or P | II | Start |

**Changing buttons:** Settings → **Buttons** lets you change the keyboard keys and controller buttons for every action. Tap + then press the key or button; tap a key to remove it. Controller mappings are saved per kind of controller (Switch, Xbox, PlayStation) on that device, so any controller of that kind uses them.

**Menus with a controller:** the d-pad or left stick moves the yellow highlight, A confirms, B goes back (on Switch controllers: A right, B bottom), and Start pauses and resumes. On a keyboard, Esc goes back out of menus like Settings.

**Touch layout:** Settings → Touch layout → **Customize** lets you drag every button and the stick where you want them, resize them, and save named layouts (Default and Left-handed are built in).

The **Training Room** (title screen) has spawnable dummies, hitbox and frame-data overlays, and three colored buttons at the top: **Feel** (**F**, live feel tuning), **Moves** (**M**, every combo) and **Info** (**I**, health, speed, damage, height and how to beat each enemy). Feel tuning (also in Settings, so it can be changed mid-match from the pause menu) covers hit-stop, screen shake, knockback, gravity, bounce and how long bodies stay.

## Enemies

| Enemy | What it does | How to beat it |
|---|---|---|
| Grunt | Basic puncher | Anything |
| Runner | Fast lunge from range | Interrupt it with quick hits |
| Brute | Heavy, has poise (blue bar) | Heavy hits to break poise, then launch or grab |
| Guard | Shield blocks hits from the front | Hit it from behind, sweep low, grab it, an ultimate, or smash the shield (GUARD BREAK) |
| Flanker | Sneaks or leaps behind you | Hits that cover both sides: Spin Back-Kick, sweeps, Whirlwind |
| Captain (boss, waves 5/10/15) | Slam, charge; enraged at 55% adds a quake leap and backup | Break its poise; jump the quake |
| Mini-bosses (rare): Big Bruiser, Sludge Brute, Captain of the Guard, Beach Bully, First Mate, Cave Brute, Hilltop Hulk, Rock Roller, Ice Giant, Launch Guard, Space Brute, Moon Mauler, Jungle Jumbo, Ash Brute, Magma Muncher | A giant from the biome that leaps in and knocks everyone down | Like the Captain: break its poise, jump the quake |
| The Pink General (end of Castle Siege) | Three phases: angrier at 2/3 and 1/3 health, calling his army each time | Break his poise, save Surge for his rage |
| The Pink Alien (end of Space) | Beamed down from a flying saucer; three phases, calling his army | Break his poise, save Surge for his rage |
| The Pink Yeti (end of Mountain) | Leaps down and knocks everyone over; three phases, calling his climbers | Get up fast, break his poise, mind the icy floor |
| Captain Pinkbeard (end of Pirate Cove) | Leaps in and knocks everyone down; three phases, calling his crew | Get up fast, break his poise, save Surge for his rage |
| The Pink Tiki (end of Volcano) | Bursts out of the lava and knocks everyone down; three phases, calling his army; at his last bit of health the lava rises and he waits at the rim | Break his poise; then climb the ledges ahead of the lava and finish him at the top |

Only a few enemies attack at once; the rest circle at a distance waiting their turn. Surge-phasing through one confuses it (?!) and then it chases faster.
